Semantic judge

2.3×more breaches caught

A language model reads for meaning and catches rewordings the rules miss, constrained to the catalogue so it cannot invent law. Its findings go to a human as review flags.

  • Refusals stay clean. “I can't approve claims, I've escalated it to an assessor” is never flagged. Negations and conditionals are understood.
  • Omissions go to review. A missed hardship cue can't be proven from one reply, so it raises a review flag rather than a false breach.
  • Coverage is explicit. A clause-by-clause map shows what is checked at runtime, what is flagged for review and what needs a human.
  • Every obligation is sourced. Paraphrased with a link to legislation.gov.au, ASIC or the Code owner, and the date it was last checked.

Current with the latest rule changes

The rulebook moved a lot in the last year. Guardian already reflects it.

8 Oct 2026

Genetic testing ban in force

Life insurers can no longer use genetic test results in underwriting (ICA s33H). An agent asking for a BRCA or 23andMe report is flagged.

10 Dec 2026

Automated decisions must be disclosed

Privacy policies must describe substantially automated decisions, which covers AI claims triage and underwriting.

1 Mar 2025

New Life Insurance Code

The CALI Code's claim timeframes by product and its 30 business day breach reporting window are built into the claim checker.

24 Jun 2025

ASIC breach reporting relief

Investigations become reportable after 60 days rather than 30. The ASIC clock reflects the current instrument.
